The objective is to keep market logic, signal generation, risk management, and trade execution as clearly separated components whenever the strategy permits. --- ## 1. Requirements Analysis Before implementation, the trading requirements are translated into explicit technical rules. Typical requirements include: - Entry conditions - Exit conditions - Market direction - Timeframe relationships - Position rules - Risk constraints - Execution frequency - State transitions - Reset conditions Ambiguous strategy descriptions are converted into deterministic conditions before coding. --- ## 2.
